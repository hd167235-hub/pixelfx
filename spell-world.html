Đảo Phép Thuật

You are a wizard washed up on an island at dusk. Your staff still works. Walk the coast, light the stones, and find the gate.

Move

  • WASD walk
  • Shift run
  • drag orbit the camera
  • wheel zoom
  • QE turn the camera

Cast

  • click cast at the crosshair
  • 1–6 pick a spell
  • Shift+1..0 the rest of the book
  • Space blink C poof

The book

Effects are played live from the pixelfx library — 30-odd spells, every one a small piece of code. The island, the wizard and the sea are three.js. H closes this card.
